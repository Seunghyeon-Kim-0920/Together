Natural Earth states that all raster and vector map data on its website are in
the public domain and may be used and modified for any purpose. Attribution is
not required, but Together displays “Map data: Natural Earth” (translated to the
selected interface language) as a courtesy.

Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Transformation performed for Together:
- ESRI Shapefile land polygons converted to a compact SVG path.
- Coordinates projected with an equirectangular WGS84 mapping into the
  1000 × 500 SVG viewBox used by Together's city markers.
- Coordinate precision rounded to three SVG user-space decimals.

Transformation performed for Together:
- GeoJSON LineString and MultiLineString country boundaries converted to one
  compact SVG path without country labels or unrelated map features.
- Coordinates projected with the same equirectangular WGS84 mapping into the
  1000 × 500 SVG viewBox used by the land layer and city markers.
- Dateline discontinuities split instead of drawing across the whole map.
- Coordinate precision rounded to three SVG user-space decimals.
